# Hide Blocked Messages for Discord

A Chrome extension that completely hides messages from people you've blocked
on Discord in the browser: the "1 blocked message — Show message" bars, and
the "Blocked message" line above replies to them.

## Install

1. Click the green **Code** button above → **Download ZIP**, and unzip it
   somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. Documents).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the **`extension`** folder.
4. Refresh Discord.

To update later, replace the folder with a new download (same place, same
name) and click the extension's reload icon in `chrome://extensions`.

## A blocked message is showing again?

Click the extension icon (🧩 → **Hide Blocked Messages**) → **Fix it** → click
the message → **OK**. To undo, click the icon → **Undo my fixes**.

If your Discord isn't in English, do this once on a "Blocked message" reply
line.

## Hide replies to blocked people too

By default, when someone you didn't block replies to someone you did, only
the "Blocked message" line above their reply is hidden. To hide the whole
reply, click the extension icon and tick **Also hide replies to blocked
people**.

## Is it safe?

Yes. It only runs on discord.com, can't read your messages or account, and
never sends anything anywhere. Its one permission, **storage**, just
remembers your fixes.

Works in Chrome, not the Discord desktop app. Not affiliated with Discord.
